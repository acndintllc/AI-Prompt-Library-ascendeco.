## Introduction

Welcome to **The Ultimate AI System Prompt Library**, the most comprehensive public collection of system prompts from major artificial intelligence models. This book contains leaked, documented, and reverse-engineered system prompts that reveal how AI assistants are instructed to behave, respond, and interact with users.

### What Are System Prompts?

System prompts are the hidden instructions given to AI models before they interact with users. They define:
- The AI's identity and capabilities
- Behavioral guidelines and constraints
- Response formatting requirements
- Safety and ethical boundaries
- Knowledge cutoff dates
- Task-specific instructions

### Why This Collection Matters

Understanding system prompts helps you:
1. **Better interact with AI** - Know what your AI assistant is designed to do
2. **Improve your prompting** - Learn from professional prompt engineering
3. **Understand AI limitations** - See what constraints are built into each model
4. **Compare AI approaches** - See how different companies design their assistants
5. **Research and education** - Study AI behavior and instruction patterns

## Appendix A: Prompt Engineering Best Practices

### Common Patterns Across Models
1. Identity Establishment
2. Knowledge Boundaries
3. Behavioral Guidelines
4. Formatting Requirements

### Best Practices
1. Be clear and detailed
2. Use positive and negative examples
3. Encourage step-by-step reasoning
4. Request specific formatting
5. Specify desired length or format

---

## Appendix B: Glossary

- **System Prompt**: Initial instructions defining AI behavior
- **Knowledge Cutoff**: Training data end date
- **Hallucination**: AI-generated false information
- **Token**: Text unit (~4 characters)
- **Fine-tuning**: Additional specialized training
- **Zero-shot Prompting**: No examples provided
- **Few-shot Prompting**: Examples included
- **Chain of Thought**: Step-by-step reasoning
- **Temperature**: Output randomness control
- **Agentic AI**: Autonomous action-taking AI
